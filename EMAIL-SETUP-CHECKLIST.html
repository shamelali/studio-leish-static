Cloudflare Email Routing Setup

📧 Setup Checklist

1 Open Cloudflare Dashboard

Click the button below to open your Cloudflare dashboard:

Open Cloudflare →

Login and select leish.my site

2 Enable Email Routing

  • Navigate to Email → Email Routing (left sidebar)
  • Click "Enable Email Routing" button
  • Click "Get started" if prompted

3 Add Destination Address

This is where forwarded emails will go:

leishstudio.main@gmail.com
  • Go to Destination addresses tab
  • Click "Add destination address"
  • Enter: leishstudio.main@gmail.com
  • Check inbox and click verification link

⚠️ You MUST verify this email before proceeding

4 Create Email Routes

Go to Routes tab and add these:

✉️ hello@leish.my → leishstudio.main@gmail.com
✉️ bookings@leish.my → leishstudio.main@gmail.com
✉️ studio@leish.my → leishstudio.main@gmail.com
✉️ admin@leish.my → leishstudio.main@gmail.com
  • Click "Catch-all address" tab
  • Enable catch-all → Forward to leishstudio.main@gmail.com

5 Verify Setup

After completing above, send test emails to:

echo "Test" | mail -s "Test hello" hello@leish.my # Or just send from your personal email

Check leishstudio.main@gmail.com inbox - emails should arrive within 5 minutes.
